## BASS-II experiment selection · hunter-vz/flame-in-freefall

The team takes NASA's BASS-II free-text flow notes seriously. It reconstructs the order of fan settings within a test and calibrates by day and restrictor. It keeps the whole test together when holding data out. Its four-row registry even leaves a probability blank for a pressure the team cannot learn from those experiments. A claim that FIRST FIRE invented the idea of a registered next test would miss this work. **A judge can check the outcome definition.** In FIRST FIRE’s Atlas, open BASS-II O12, source row 62. The row gives initial oxygen of 13.6% and final oxygen of 13.5%. The note reports failed ignition at all four fan settings. O14, source row 65, describes a flame extinguishing as flow fell and omits the flow at extinction. The competing notebook preserves these subtypes in its transcription, then combines failed ignition and blowoff as class zero. Its separate whole-test validation reports 6/9 class-zero settings detected, including four failed ignitions in O12. Give FIRST FIRE a judge-visible exercise that asks for the number of independent tests, the specific endpoint, and the missing quantity before showing any probability. The original source establishes that O12 had one test with four settings. **A second test concerns the score.** The notebook’s acquisition replay compares its selection with random and chronological orders. Its function `brier_on_full` scores all 60 points at every step, including any already revealed for training. If FIRST FIRE adds a replay, freeze an independent set of whole tests before selecting anything and publish the error on that fixed set after each choice. The competitor’s headline of 11.2 versus 17.9 tests is the number needed to approach its full-data fitted score in a historical replay. Actual specimen savings require a separate study. ## FLAMEWISE catalogue and import · haneenhassan042-star/falconx-nasa-space-apps

FLAMEWISE lets a reader distinguish a direct official record from a catalogue lookup or an entry awaiting verification. Its landing page distinguishes catalogue entries from imported measurement rows. That keeps a parent programme and its sub-investigations from quietly becoming a flight-test count. The initial archive has 31 catalogue entries and zero imported measurements. **A judge can check blank versus measured zero.** In FLAMEWISE, `numericFields` correctly looks for a populated numeric value before suggesting a column. Once selected, `renderChart` applies `Number` to every row, which converts an empty CSV string to zero when a different row supplies a numeric value. FIRST FIRE’s Transport Lab rejects an empty sensor cell and accepts a literal `0`. Try a valid six-observation-or-longer trace twice, changing just one reported zero to a blank. The two imports should give different results. This is an existing, observable distinction a judge can check offline.
