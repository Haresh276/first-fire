# FIRST FIRE: first five-minute observation

**Blank protocol. No participant results have been recorded.** One session can show what happened for one person; it cannot establish a learning or time-saving benefit.

Invite one volunteer who did not build FIRST FIRE. Let them choose [English](https://first-fire-lunar-lab.christsamuel26.chatgpt.site/classroom.html?case=1&lang=en) or [Telugu](https://first-fire-lunar-lab.christsamuel26.chatgpt.site/classroom.html?case=1&lang=te). Ask whether you may record their task outcome anonymously. If they decline, offer the exercise without taking notes.

**Read aloud:** “This room experiment lists an alarm activation time and two instrument readings. Use FIRST FIRE to find the place in the instrument record where those readings match. Show me what you used. What do the two clocks let you conclude?”

Start timing when they can see the case. Give them up to five minutes and let them talk through the work. If they ask for help, note the prompt and continue. A timeout, wrong answer, or confusing control is useful evidence.
