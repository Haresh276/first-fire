# Strong local chat calibration plan

Bank A is exposed development data after the immutable first run. The training material consists of the stronger model's normal browser answers on the original 100 and A's 100. Assign each ID within its bank and difficulty level to training or validation by sorting SHA-256 of "FF-reply-confidence-2/" plus the bank and ID. The first floor(0.70 × stratum size) train the model. The remainder stay held out. No feature, weight or threshold will be selected from bank B.

The target is the correctness of the entire answer shown, including whether a decision to abstain was appropriate. A revised A answer gets a new semantic verdict unless its complete visible answer and sources are unchanged. The older bank retains its original wording-sensitive machine rubric. Estimate separate intercepts for the two grading methods and shared slopes (sum of logistic losses, L2 gradient coefficient 4 on slopes). At inference use the A semantic intercept. Report validation by bank and distinguish their grading methods. The constant test comparator is the semantic training-split pass proportion. There is no validation-split refitting. Bank B is an untouched one-shot test.

Inference reads only the final verified turn. Predeclared signals are verified synthesis; quantitative receipt or checked expression; abstention or clarification; content-word coverage; coverage of named test IDs; explicit requested calculation without a checked computation; relational or multi-part wording; a targeted, explained refutation; and fraction of synthesis blocks with a source reference. These signals cannot establish truth. Use a numeric estimate for scoring every local-mode completion, including withheld replies and sparse routes. It is withheld from the archive-only interface and from other model versions. For the tested local model show a percentage rounded to the nearest five points only when at least 12 semantic training examples used that reply route, at least 10 semantic training replies and 8 held-out semantic replies landed in its probability band, and that validation band's confidence/accuracy gap was no more than 10 percentage points. Otherwise show that this kind of reply has insufficient calibration evidence.

Use bands [0, .5), [.5, .8), [.8, 1]. Report mean predicted and actual pass rate (and signed difference), all-row Brier and log loss, Brier of the fixed training prevalence, expected calibration error and maximum populated-band gap, denominators, 95% Wilson intervals for observed accuracy, high-confidence misses, and UI numeric coverage. Every populated band must be within ten percentage points to meet the calibration criterion. Empty or small groups cannot validate a probability. The UI validation screen can select the display policy but cannot validate that policy. Only the independent test can do that.
