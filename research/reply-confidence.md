# How to read reply confidence

Open **Evidence checks** under a chat reply to see its estimated chance of passing an answer review. A reviewer checks whether the reply answers the actual question, uses supporting sources and handles its limits correctly. A refusal can pass a question about a missing measurement and fail a question that the archive can answer. A forecast reply can contain two percentages. The probability of sustained spread belongs to the physical model. Reply confidence describes how reliably the chat answers the research question.

## How the estimate is made

The local evidence gate first checks the proposed answer and decides what to display. The confidence estimator then reads the final text, its citations and the completed evidence checks. The answer and the evidence decision stay the same at every confidence level. A verified passage can still address the wrong experiment.

The estimator uses a logistic regression with eight inputs. Four identify a quantitative receipt, an independently reviewed interpretation, a quotation-only response or a withheld answer. The other four record an explained scope limit, the fraction of distinct question terms reused in the answer, wording that requests a comparison or multiple answers, and the number of sources up to three. These are imperfect signals. For example, repeating a material name improves word overlap without establishing that an experiment used it. None of the inputs read answer keys, question IDs, difficulty labels or the old confidence number.

The training set is the original 100 development questions. Each whole reply receives its original rubric's binary verdict. We center the eight inputs and fit the intercept and weights by minimizing summed log loss with a quadratic penalty on the weights (coefficient 2 in the gradient). The intercept is unpenalized. The 24 historical follow-up questions are evaluated separately. They had appeared in earlier releases. The plan fixes the inputs, penalty, bins and scoring rules before opening the fresh test answers. The fit file records the development bank, response file and inference code hashes. The reply carries the model identifier and the probability recorded before grading.

## How to judge it

Report accuracy, mean predicted probability, Brier score and log loss on every evaluated reply. The Brier score averages the squared difference between each probability and its binary outcome. A miss at 90% confidence costs 0.81 and a success at 90% costs 0.01. Log loss gives greater weight to confident mistakes. A useful comparison assigns every reply the development pass rate and measures that constant forecast on exactly the same test questions.

The three fixed groups are below 50%, 50% through just below 80%, and 80% or above. In each group, compare mean confidence with the observed pass frequency. The reliability table includes the number of questions and a 95% Wilson interval for the observed pass frequency. Expected calibration error weights each absolute gap by the number of replies in its group. The recorded acceptance criterion requires every populated group's gap to be at most ten percentage points. Empty groups supply no evidence and small groups have wide intervals. Checking the highest-confidence misses and the performance on withheld answers helps interpret the average.

The original development rubric grades the text rendered in the browser and checks required facts and contradictions with normalized regular expressions. It also checks the reply status and its citation families, titles and locators. A correct paraphrase or alternate primary source can lose credit. A requested phrase can appear in a tangential answer. As a control, an empty refusal passed 0/100 development and 0/24 follow-up items. A generic refusal saying “There is insufficient evidence” passed 8/100 and 7/24 respectively. Every one of those items had an unanswerable reference and did not require a citation. The fresh independent review accepts equivalent units, reasoning and supporting sources, and checks for material false statements throughout the reply. The resulting score tests transfer across questions and grading methods.

## Reproduce the estimate

Capture the final visible outputs on the two previously exposed banks with `scripts/run_local_chat_exam.cjs`. Fit with `node scripts/fit_reply_confidence.cjs --traces development.jsonl followup.jsonl`. The fitter verifies the original question-bank hashes and each captured question before using its rubric. It writes the prediction coefficients to `site/answer-confidence-fit.js` and the development and follow-up reliability tables to `research/chat-runs/reply-confidence-development.json`. Run `node --test tests/reply-confidence.test.cjs` to check abstentions, final-turn scoring, empty bins and exact probability boundaries.
