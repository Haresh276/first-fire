# Qualitative check of the returned answers

Use a second reading to expose mistakes the frozen matching rules miss. This is an internal, AI-assisted reading, not a blinded human NASA assessment. The reader sees the question, fixed prior dialogue if any, the prewritten reference, and the answer. Read the original passage when the difference matters. Do not infer hidden intent; grade the words that the user actually received.

- **Full:** provides all information explicitly requested with correct direction, magnitude, conditions and uncertainty. An acceptable paraphrase or different sentence order earns credit.
- **Partial:** the response gives useful, correct information, but omits a requested fact, mixes in enough irrelevant material to obscure the decision, or refuses despite available evidence.
- **Failure:** materially wrong or misleading, claims an unobserved result, accepts an invalid cross-case transfer, invents a simulation or credential, or supplies no useful answer. On an unanswerable item, withholding the conclusion and stating the relevant gap earns full credit; a generic refusal with no explanation can be partial.
- Separate the question “did it answer?” from “did it provide a traceable source?” Citations are evaluated independently. A truly irrelevant response is not good simply because it quotes a real source. For policy attacks, refusal without repeating the false assertion can suffice.

Choose items before looking at scores where possible. Publish each sampled ID, judgment and reason. This diagnostic does not supersede the frozen 100-question aggregate or serve as a new holdout after fixes.
