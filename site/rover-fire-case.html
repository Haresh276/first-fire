What supports this fire case?

Model boundaries and source files

FLINT calibrates a thin-solid extinction law. A thick window, local smoke plume or flame spreading against the flow needs different evidence. With orbit fans off, FLINT cannot calculate oxygen transport by diffusion. With orbit fans on, the solid limits are concurrent-flow extrapolations. BASS and Saffire supply the flight observations.

Gas doses assume instantaneous mixing at fixed cabin volume. The model has no detector, pressure-hull rating or crew physiology model. A vent-while-burning experiment at lunar gravity would test the suppression thresholds.
