How it works

What the decoder assumes

  • FLINT 1.1 simulates each burn in FM²'s sealed 14.4 L chamber at 8.2 psia, treating the strip as aramid-like fabric (Synergy Pro Navy) and the gas as well mixed. Each burn starts in fresh gas.
  • For every possible limit from 18% to 36% (in 0.1-point steps) it predicts the four burn lengths, then weighs each limit by how well its lengths match yours. The range shown covers 80% of that weight.
  • "Oxygen bookkeeping only" ignores the CO₂ and steam the flame makes. The difference between the two answers is the sealed-box correction.
  • FLINT is not reviewed by NASA. Our own predictions for FM² are sealed in the forecast register; this decoder is how we will score them.
