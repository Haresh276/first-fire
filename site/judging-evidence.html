Best Use of Science

Across 14 flight runs, holding out a whole smoke material gave a mean absolute error of 1.25 V for the heater-setting model and 3.25 V for the pooled-mean baseline. Three Kapton runs still fell on the wrong side of the study’s 2 V reference. Inspect the run-level errors.

Best Use of Data

Try asking for 85% oxygen in a burner and then in a cabin. Follow an accepted or rejected match to its original table, cell and measurement location. Take the source trail with you as JSON.

Reproduce the 106 burner-only matches and the SAME-R run count · Export a question-specific brief

Best Use of Technology

Ask the research desk a question about a flight record. Follow the cited row, change the physical condition, and look for a justified refusal. The benchmark includes incorrect answers that can be inspected individually.
