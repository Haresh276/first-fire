# Rover evidence by space agency

The 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge names 17 partner agencies alongside NASA. In this register, a flight measurement belongs to the instrument, place and time of the observation. A design value belongs to the named hardware configuration. Laboratory and ground-station figures can inform tests or operations while the corresponding rover parameter may still be unknown. [Official partner roster](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/space-agency-partners/) · [Archived roster](evidence/files/partner-roster-2026.html)

The closest references for an occupied lunar rover are JAXA’s pressurized habitat and NASA’s open, suit-dependent transport designs. ISRO’s Pragyan, NASA’s Perseverance and instruments on Curiosity and Perseverance supply flown robotic precedents. ESA’s drill and MBRSC’s wheel provide ground-test results with documented test conditions. The other entries identify the programmes, components or engineering work that each source can support.

## NASA · United States · National Aeronautics and Space Administration

Three different vehicles answer three design questions. Perseverance’s flight hardware gives a 1,025 kg, six-wheel reference for robotic mobility. The planned lunar terrain vehicles accommodate two suited astronauts on an open deck. For fire research, Saffire V sample 2 was a cotton jersey specimen in an uncrewed spacecraft in orbit. At 70.7 kPa, 26.2 mol% oxygen and 20 cm/s fan flow, the reported steady flame size was 10 cm. Applying that measurement to a rover cabin would require matching the gravity, atmosphere, material and flow. [Perseverance body and wheels specifications](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-2020-perseverance/rover-components/) · [Lunar terrain vehicles](https://www.nasa.gov/moonbase-phases/) · [Saffire V, Table 2 and Fig. 3](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20210011521/downloads/ICES%202021-266%200503-2021%20final.pdf)

## GGPEN · Angola · National Space Programme Management Office

The mission control centre monitors ANGOSAT-2 and describes capacity for three satellites at once. Its continuous telemetry and fault diagnosis illustrate the ground crew’s role in rover operations. No rover fault-response time or lunar radio specification appears in the centre’s description. [GGPEN mission control centre, capabilities and functions](https://ggpen.gov.ao/public/index.php/solucoes/mcc)

## CONAE · Argentina · National Commission on Space Activities

The joint CLTC–CONAE Neuquén station supported Chinese lunar missions and Tianwen-1. Its 35 m ground antenna receives at 2.2–2.3 GHz and 8.4–8.5 GHz. These bands can inform a ground receiver compatibility check. The records leave the rover-to-lander radio and total data rate unspecified. [Argentina’s mission support account](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/noticias/filmus-visito-la-estacion-de-espacio-profundo-cltc-conae-neuquen-un-puente-de-cooperacion) · [Neuquén equipment, §6(a)](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/node/182397)

## BSA · Bahrain · Bahrain Space Agency

Bahrain identified four navigation cameras for the UAE’s Rashid Rover 3 and subsequently reported that its engineers had developed them in-house. This identifies a specific partner-built rover payload. Camera field of view, stereo geometry and measured lunar navigation performance are absent from the reports. [Bahrain’s 2024 submission, p. 6 §1(c)](https://documents.un.org/doc/undoc/gen/v24/076/85/pdf/v2407685.pdf) · [2025 progress report, p. 3](https://documents.un.org/api/symbol/access?l=en&s=A%2FAC.105%2F1367&t=pdf)

## AEB · Brazil · Brazilian Space Agency

AEB documents spacecraft integration and environmental testing at INPE’s LIT. INPE specifies an ultimate vacuum of 10⁻⁷ mbar and a −196 to +150 °C operating range for each of six shroud zones in its large chamber. Those are facility capabilities for planning thermal tests. The sources report no operating-temperature limit for a rover. [AEB management report, p. 61](https://www.gov.br/aeb/pt-br/canais_atendimento/ouvidoria/RelatorioDeGestaoAEB2023.pdf) · [INPE thermal-vacuum facilities, item (a)](https://www.gov.br/inpe/pt-br/area-conhecimento/montagem-integracao-e-testes/laboratorio-vacuo-termico)

## CSA · Canada · Canadian Space Agency

CSA supplied Curiosity’s arm-mounted Alpha Particle X-ray Spectrometer. For a head placed close to rock or soil, CSA describes roughly 5–10 minutes for a rapid elemental survey and 2–3 hours for a full analysis. These times help budget a sampling stop. Motor power and complete payload energy remain unspecified. [CSA, APXS measurement sequence](https://www.canada.ca/en/news/archive/2012/08/canada-contribution-mars-science-laboratory.html)

## ESA · Europe · European Space Agency

ESA specifies a 2 m drill reach for the six-wheel Rosalind Franklin rover. Its gravity-offloaded ground twin recovered a sample at 1.7 m in cemented clay on a 7° platform in Turin. The measured depth supports planning a terrestrial sampling demonstration. Performance in Martian soil remains unverified. [ESA rover mission, Drilling in brief](https://explorationscience.esa.int/project/exomars-rosalind-franklin-rfm/) · [ESA ground test, Drill operations](https://www.esa.int/articles/first-deep-drilling-success-for-exomars)

## ISRO · India · Indian Space Research Organisation

Chandrayaan-3’s Pragyan lunar rover provides a compact comparison for electrical power and data relay. ISRO lists its mass as 26 kg, its power generation as 50 W and its communications path through the lander. Deriving battery energy in Wh would require generation over time, loads and storage data. [ISRO, Overall specifications and Rover payloads](https://www.isro.gov.in/Chandrayaan3_Details.html)

## ASI · Italy · Italian Space Agency

Italy contributed the Ma_MISS spectrometer for ESA’s Rosalind Franklin drilling system, with scientific participation from INAF-IAPS. Its location inside the drill allows it to observe the exposed borehole wall. This defines a payload placement and viewing requirement. Calibration data from a Martian borehole are unavailable in the design description. [ASI ExoMars, Italian contribution and drilling system](https://www.asi.it/en/planets-stars-universe/solar-system-and-beyond/exomars/)

## JAXA · Japan · Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency

JAXA and Toyota’s 2019 crewed lunar concept proposed 13 m³ of living space for two occupants. In 2026 a JAXA engineer described cabin pressure regulation, CO₂ removal and active thermal control. Occupied living space helps size the layout. A gas-inventory model also needs the free gas volume, pressure, oxygen fraction and temperature, which these records do not establish. [Joint concept proposal, March 2019](https://global.toyota/en/newsroom/corporate/27059582.html) · [JAXA interview, thermal and environmental control section](https://humans-in-space.jaxa.jp/space-job/specialist/voice/detail/13.html)

## KASA · Republic of Korea · Korea AeroSpace Administration

KASA’s June 2026 mission discussion envisaged a daytime rover operating window of up to 14 Earth days. Route planning must account for available energy. A separate 2017 KICT terrestrial wheel study compared drawbar pull, torque and sinkage at 10 mm/s over imposed slip ratios of 0.1–0.4. The KICT tests predate KASA and supply no measured endurance or traction for Korea’s proposed flight rover. [KASA operating concept, June 2026](https://www.kasa.go.kr/prog/plcyBrf/brief/kor/sub01_01_04/view.do?plcyBrfNo=427) · [KICT wheel experiment, Tables 2–4 and §4](https://kscejournal.or.kr/jksce/XmlViewer/f406872)

## NASRDA · Nigeria · National Space Research and Development Agency

Nigeria’s Centre for Space Transport and Propulsion describes integrating a rocket for an April 2019 flight test and making final checks on its launch platform. The sequence provides an example of independent integration and quality checks. Sections 2 and 4 give no rover loads, vibration spectra or wheel data. [NASRDA technical divisions, §§2 and 4](https://www.nasrda.gov.ng/center-for-space-transport-and-propulsion/)

## AEP · Paraguay · Paraguayan Space Agency

AEP’s GuaraniSat-2 drawing lists 3.94 kg and a 110.4 × 112 × 341.5 mm stowed envelope. Its separate deployed drawing illustrates what an interface specification should identify before combining components. These dimensions and the stated mass describe a CubeSat design. The source gives no planetary rover envelope or structural load rating. [AEP mechanical properties and deployed view, pp. 2–3](https://aep.gov.py/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/GUARANISAT-2_Structure-20250717_compressed.pdf)

## ASES · Senegal · Senegalese Space Studies Agency

ASES’s notice documents its research, policy and training mandate. Separately, the national SENSAT programme describes Senegalese engineers’ work with the University Space Centre of Montpellier on the GAINDESAT-1A Earth-orbiting CubeSat, launched in 2024. It is an integration and operations reference. The reviewed records supply no ASES rover specification or cabin experiment. [ASES notice, p. 36](https://aps.sn/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/MAGAZINE-APS-n6.pdf) · [SENSAT, GAINDESAT-1A mission and technical description](https://sensat.mesrisenegal.sn/public/nos-satellites/gaindesat-1a)

## AEE · Spain · Spanish Space Agency

AEE credits Spain’s Mars weather instruments. The CAB (CSIC–INTA) team that led MEDA reported −83 to −22 °C and a gust of about 35.4 km/h outside Perseverance at Jezero on sols 43–44. These are measured local conditions for an exposed sensor. They give neither an occupied cabin temperature nor a mission-wide thermal design range. [AEE bilateral programmes, NASA collaboration](https://www.aee.gob.es/eu/Actividades/Bilaterales.html) · [CAB weather report, pp. 1–3](https://cab.inta-csic.es/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/np_2021_04_06_parte_meteorologico_meda.pdf)

## TUA · Türkiye · Turkish Space Agency

TUA’s performance plan places preliminary work on a mobile lunar rover in the AYAP-2 soft-landing phase. Its September 2026 integration update addresses the first-phase spacecraft. The plan establishes the mobility objective. The cited records contain no rover mass, operating speed or measured surface performance. [2025 performance plan, printed p. 16, indicator 2](https://cdn.tua.gov.tr/679399ed1cd1a.pdf) · [TUA first-phase integration update, 16 September 2026](https://tua.gov.tr/tr/haberler/turkiye-nin-ay-yolculugunda-geri-sayim-basladi)

## MBRSC · United Arab Emirates · Mohammed Bin Rashid Space Centre

Rashid 1 was an approximately 10 kg robot aboard HAKUTO-R, whose April 2023 landing attempt failed. A terrestrial single-wheel experiment later reported traction tests under 24.5 N over controlled slip from 10% to 50%. The experimental wheel’s grousers were 20 mm high, versus 15 mm on the flight wheel. The rig provides a repeatable test for comparing wheel designs. The mission yielded no surface traction measurement. [Dubai government, landing outcome and flight dimensions](https://www.protocol.dubai.ae/en/media-listing/news-events/mohammed-bin-rashid-visits-mbrsc-announces-rashid-2-new-emirati-lunar-mission/) · [Hurrell et al., single-wheel experiment, §2.1](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11214-025-01164-8)

## UKSA · United Kingdom · UK Space Agency

Airbus built ESA’s Rosalind Franklin rover in Stevenage. UKSA funded the Aberystwyth-led Enfys instrument for choosing drill targets. UKSA describes the Stevenage Mars Yard as a 30 × 13 m test arena containing 300 tonnes of sand. These figures define a terrestrial navigation test facility. A rover climbing limit would require a load- and terrain-specific test. [UKSA, Airbus and Enfys responsibilities](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/mission-to-explore-life-on-mars-one-step-closer-as-uk-team-to-replace-russian-components-for-mars-rover) · [UKSA, Airbus Mars Yard opening and dimensions](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/airbus-defence-and-space-opens-state-of-the-art-mars-yard)
