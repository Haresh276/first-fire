# NASA PSI data audit

On 14 November 2026 we retrieved the public NASA PSI metadata for 19 investigations and all 20 experimental CSV files exposed by those records. The local archive preserves every CSV byte. Its manifest preserves the public metadata fields used here, the full metadata response hash, file and investigation versions, download URLs, sizes and SHA-256 digests. The observation table contains **1,058 records from 9 investigations**. A row may represent a burn, an attempted ignition, or an aging observation. The number of unique fires cannot be inferred from that total. [NASA repository](https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo) · [NASA database guide](https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/bps/bps-general/bps-data_osdr_psi/Complete_PSI_Database_Guide.pdf)

The ten catalogue-only accessions are PSI-10, -20, -21, -22, -23, -68, -102, -106, -107 and -115. Their raw tables, where available, remain downloadable. The PSI-10 metadata and its DataCite record leave the license field blank. All other selected PSI records explicitly list CC0-1.0. The catalogue links to each DOI. ## Measuring conditions and endpoints

`record_id` is accession, table number and CSV record number joined by colons. CSV numbering counts the header as 1. `source_file`, `source_row`, `source_url` and `doi` preserve the trace to the original. The raw manifest pins each file version. Whitespace and source spellings remain in originals. A blank normalized field means unavailable, inapplicable or rejected with a displayed reason. A recorded zero is distinct from a blank. Derived values carry up to eight decimal places for comparison and do not imply additional instrumental precision.

* Oxygen columns distinguish initial ambient gas, final ambient gas and oxidizer delivered through a burner. `o2_low_pct` and `o2_high_pct` preserve a reported ambient interval for Saffire or duplicate the initial ambient value for the other studies. A Saffire single approximate value is accompanied by its qualifier. Initial/final concentrations in BASS-II and normal CFI-G are temporally distinct measurements.
* Absolute pressures use 760 mmHg = 1 atm = 101.325 kPa ≈ 14.69595 psia. PSI-69 record C11M201 contains zero pressure and reports burning. Its derived pressure is blank and its source value is retained. Circumstances and undocumented uncertainty can prevent a precise comparison even when pressure is reported. 
* Orbital microgravity is coded as nominal zero g for broad scenario comparisons. No row-level accelerometer values were derived. The accessible PSI flight tables give no real lunar gravity observation here.
* Flow in cm/s uses explicitly dimensioned source values. BASS-II fan displays, ACME mass flow and optical camera frame counts remain distinct from airflow velocity. A SAME-R inlet velocity describes smoke generation, not the airflow over a spreading flame.
* `phase` describes the tested fuel or detection phenomenon. SAME-R uses `aerosol`; fuel phase alone is insufficient to compare fire behavior across materials. The Saffire CSV sample detail gives thickness × length × width in the listed order. A missing material name is never filled through a guess based on the previous row.
* `burn_time_s`, `hot_ignition`, `termination_code`, an actual natural extinction time, and a smoke heating duration are different measurements. CFI-G's FT denotes stopping the flow, SE denotes self-extinction. Only explicit burn times become numeric, with a qualifier when the source gives an approximation. 
* The FLEX CSV column headings drop gas-name subscripts and label the burning-rate unit as mm. The experimental report's Table IX identifies O₂, CO₂, N₂ and rate in mm²/s. Gas species mappings use that report. We leave the disputed rate unit out of the normalized table. Some source compositions do not sum to one; no missing fraction is redistributed. The technical report discusses possible contamination for the disruptive tests in section 6. [Dietrich et al., Table IX and appendix](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20150023456)

## Consequential counterexamples

**Visible extinction.** FLEX-155 (C03H201; PSI-69 CSV row 156) used heptane at 764.9 mmHg in an 18% O₂, 67% N₂, 15% CO₂ chamber on the ISS. The droplet began at 2.73 mm and measured 2.52 mm at visible extinction. The CSV reports 2.7 s and an extinction endpoint. The investigator's Figure 159 (printed p.183) describes fast vaporization continuing afterward, an eventual plateau and a vapor cloud, evidence for cool-flame burning after visible loss. Figure 124 for C12H201/FLEX-120 describes an inconclusive post-visible observation despite a vapor cloud. Both rows say “Extinction.” A row-level endpoint lookup alone cannot classify a cool flame. The two documents trace the same experiment. [FLEX dataset](https://doi.org/10.60555/mbq8-0451) · [NASA report, Figs. 124 and 159](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20150023456/downloads/20150023456.pdf) · [FLEX investigators' peer-reviewed analysis](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12217-014-9372-2)

**Joining trials.** PSI-69 CSV row 157 assigns C03H401 to number 156. The NASA report's Table IX assigns that same identifier and timestamp to FLEX-157. Use the engineering identifier, time, fuel and conditions to cross-check joins, preserve the native IDs for both sources, and mark dubious matches for review. [FLEX dataset](https://doi.org/10.60555/mbq8-0451) · [NASA report, Table IX, printed p.24](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20150023456/downloads/20150023456.pdf)

**Burner versus habitat.** In CFI-G inverse CSV rows 18–19, 21202 A1 and A2 share 85% oxygen in the burner stream, 6% initial ethane in the chamber and a source pressure labeled approximately 2.2066 bar. The first records no hot ignition at 0.931 mg/s O₂. The second records hot ignition, 2 seconds and SE at 1.862 mg/s. This pair cannot determine a unique ignition threshold. The thesis labels the condition 2.0 atm; retain the two reported pressure values rather than forcing agreement. Compare normal CSV row 2, 21088A1, with measured pre-test chamber oxygen of 39.39%. All are gas tests. At a 34% cutoff, naively pooling inlet and ambient oxygen adds 106 false chamber matches to 113 legitimate initial chamber rows in these tables. 79 inverse rows use the 85% burner stream. Initial O₂ alone does not make the normal gas rows directly applicable to fabric in lunar gravity. [CFI-G dataset](https://doi.org/10.60555/cyt1-kh64) · [Kim thesis, method pp. 8–10 and Tables E.2/E.6](https://api.drum.lib.umd.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/278c77a3-6e86-4dc5-8413-c0381ebe74a9/content) · [CFI-G peer-reviewed paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.proci.2022.07.015)

**Initial oxygen and exposure duration.** BASS-II T28b (row 129) starts at 20.7% and records 40.4% at the end. Its paired T28a records 0.9% final oxygen. A start-of-test filter cannot take either final as the initial exposure. BASS-II's initial range is 13.6–22.2% across 129 records. Saffire I row 2 records 420 s until operators switched the flow off; the opposed burn reused the remaining fabric. Comparing durations as two independent extinction observations confounds procedure, fuel history and direction. [BASS-II](https://doi.org/10.60555/4qc4-de67) · [Saffire-I](https://doi.org/10.60555/0t15-1z43) · [Investigator report, slide 14](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20170008805)

**Beyond the selected PSI tables.** Saffire VI-2 burned 50 cm SIBAL fabric at 31.0% O₂, 54.1 kPa and 20 cm/s in orbit. Its paper provides a substantially closer atmospheric analogue to a nominal 34%/8.2 psia exploration atmosphere than Saffire I's approximately 21.5% O₂ burn. A transfer claim still needs matching fuel, geometry, flow and gravity. This published result has a different provenance from the 1,058 PSI observations. [NASA Saffire VI results, Table 1](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20240002981)

## Reproducing the snapshot

Run `python3 scripts/build_data.py --check` from the project directory to validate all original CSV digests and compare rederived CSV/JSON bytes. Run without `--check` to regenerate. To examine an updated NASA release independently, use `python3 scripts/fetch_psi.py /path/to/new-empty-snapshot`; compare versions, headers and observations before adopting it. Derived rows use only the archived files. Each primary table remains available using the DOI or versioned download URL in `data/raw/psi-manifest.json`.
