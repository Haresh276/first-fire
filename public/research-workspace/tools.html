FIRST FIREResearch desk

Research labs and tools

  • Physics lab

    Set the fuel, pressure and spread speed. Track oxygen use and heat released in a sealed cabin.

  • FM² forecast

    Inspect the four lunar fabric forecasts and score a hypothetical outcome.

  • Experiment design lab

    Try hypothetical fabric burns and choose the oxygen level for the next test.

  • Tracer transport lab

    Model the release of a tracer and measure the delay at a remote sensor.

  • Matched experiments lab

    Pair flight runs with comparable conditions and inspect how the selection changes the result.

  • Rover design lab

    Choose vehicle components, change cabin conditions and inspect the calculated budgets.

  • Rover fire experiment

    Run a matched pair of cabin trials using spread speeds measured aboard Cygnus.

  • 3D rover workbench

    Inspect a rover in 3D or drive it on lunar terrain and a Mars reference route.

  • Rover assembly

    Build a vehicle from eleven subsystems and inspect its assumed interfaces.

  • Rover test conditions

    Set the environment, cabin pressure, ventilation and crew activity.

  • Cabin tracer test

    Change fan flow or detector position and watch the modeled concentration.

  • Rover engineering results

    Read calculated loads, energy, gas losses and material quantities.

  • Rover component evidence

    Look up source pages, test conditions and limits for installed parts.

  • Rover mission review

    Compare two vehicle designs and export their assumptions and sources.

  • Field trial comparison

    Compare simulated rover motion with an independent trial.

  • Fire evidence search

    Search the reviewed flight experiments and their original sources.

  • Detector flight evidence

    Inspect smoke detector signals from NASA experiments.

  • Rover sandbox

    Explore how a sealed compartment responds to the specified conditions.

  • NASA evidence atlas

    Browse the source rows behind the research desk.
