What a judge can verify

Best Use of Science

In 18 additional ISS runs of five known materials, the previous mean for each material predicted optical response with a mean absolute error of 0.50 V. The heater model gave 1.44 V and the pooled mean gave 2.41 V. All three missed a large Kapton signal. The original 14-run study separately tested transfer to a withheld material.

Test the frozen model on 18 other ISS runs · Reproduce the original study · Check a prediction before opening the team result · Inspect the signed lunar forecast

Best Use of Data

Try asking for 85% oxygen in a burner and then in a cabin. Follow an accepted or rejected match to its original table, cell and measurement location. Take the source trail with you as JSON.

Reproduce the 106 burner-only matches and the SAME-R run count · Export a question-specific brief · Run an independent data audit
