When is another fire test justified?

Write your question first, then record what could change your decision. Allow 20 minutes. Use the source links to check the conditions yourself.

1. Match the evidence

Use the default FLEX comparison. Before revealing the outcomes, write the fuel, the two oxygen levels and two conditions you are holding approximately constant. Record both identifiers for one pair. Which additional restriction would invalidate the comparison?

2. Pick a cabin atmosphere

Compare Earth air and the exploration preset in the atmosphere designer. Use a 12 m³ volume and 29.6 kPa suit pressure. Explain how the mixture with the greater oxygen fraction can have the lower oxygen partial pressure. Record one material-specific measurement you would need before choosing an interior lining.

3. Spend one specimen

Use the FM² design lab to choose an oxygen level. Record how a sustained reading changes your next setting. Explain how an uncertain reading changes the decision. Record the signed forecast’s distinct outcome definition.

4. Check your interpretation

Which result came from a flight observation, which from a calculation, and which from an assumed threshold? Ask a partner to find one unsupported transfer. Revise your decision and attach your exported research record.
